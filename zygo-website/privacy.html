ZYGO Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 | Applicable to ZYGO Rider & ZYGO HERO Partner Apps

1. Introduction

ZYGO ("we", "our", or "us") operates the ZYGO ride-hailing and parcel delivery platform via our mobile applications and website (https://zygocab.in). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile applications on the Google Play Store and web portals.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Full name, mobile phone number, operating city, and secure password when you register as a rider or driver partner.
  • Location Data:
    • For Riders: Precise and approximate location to detect pickup spots, calculate route fares, and track ride progress.
    • For Driver Partners (Captains): Background and foreground location data to broadcast nearby ride requests, navigate pickups/drop-offs, and compute distance meters accurately.
  • Driver Verification Data: Driving License (DL), Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC), Vehicle insurance, and vehicle specifications (brand, model, plate number).
  • Transaction Data: Wallet balances, in-app top-ups, referral bonuses, and trip fare settlements.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To match riders with nearby verified driver partners.
  • To process trip payments, automated distance metering, and instant wallet settlements.
  • To ensure passenger and driver safety with live ride tracking and SOS emergency features.
  • To communicate trip status, safety updates, and customer support resolutions.

4. Data Sharing & Protection

We do not sell your personal data. Rider and driver contact numbers are masked for safety and privacy. All communication between mobile apps and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Users may request complete account deletion directly from the mobile app settings or by writing to support@zygocab.in. Upon confirmation, all personal profiles and location history are permanently expunged in compliance with applicable data protection laws.
